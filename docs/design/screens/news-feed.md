# News Feed

[← Design index](../README.md) · Route `newsFeed` · `ui/composable/newsfeed/NewsFeedScreen.kt`

A bottom-bar tab that shows `https://walkingforrochester.org/category/news/` in a full-screen embedded WebView (`WFRWebView`), inside the standard top and bottom bars. Content and styling come from the WordPress site, not from the app.

There is no screenshot because WebViews do not render in off-device previews. On a device it shows the website's news listing between the top bar ("News Feed") and the bottom bar, with News Feed selected.
